# Guide on Student Union Hiring or Selection Processes

## Background
This resources outlines ideas for Student Union Hiring or Selection Process, such as for executive positions, whether paid or volunteer.

The hiring process should be overseen by a representative hiring committee:
* 2-3 non-executive Board members
* 2 students-at-large (not Student Union student staff or volunteer, past or present)
* Student Union Professional Staff as needed

The process includes public consultation because these positions will become a "public figure," and they’ll be subject to public scrutiny, and should be thus prepared for such.

## Procedure:
1. The Board selects the board representatives for the hiring committee.
2. Student Union publicly releases hiring process (a polished version of this document) & advertises the applications for the student-at-large positions (publicly advertised) on the committee (this application will be open for 2 weeks).
3. Existing roster for the committee (board members) selects the student-at-large representatives for the committee.
4. Committee roster is made public & announced on social media & newsletter.
5. Committee reviews & approves the position description, including minimum grade average (which should be 85%), and a requirement that having additional employment in BUSU or Brock must be approved by the Board of Directors.
6. Committee reviews & approves the position application process: Cover letter must be no longer than 1 page, double spaced, min. 11pt. font; Resume must be no longer than 3 pages, min 11pt. font; The reason for these rules are for the committee to be efficient in reviewing applications, without there being an impact on quality; this bring all applicants to the same level in terms of what they’re able to share
7. Student Union releases the job posting for 3 weeks and promotes it through every communication channel.
8.	Committee decides on short list of candidates to interview
9.	Committee decides on interview questions
10.	Committee holds interviews
11.	Committee decides on 2-3 candidates to invite to make a public presentation
12.	Committee organizes public presentations
13.	Students send in feedback for a period (e.g., 1 week)
14.	Committee deliberates and makes a decision by the end of June (approximately).
15.	Student Union sends out the employment offer.
16.	Upon receiving acceptance of the selected candidate’s offer, the decision is reported in the student union newsletter and public channels (e.g., website, social media).

Student-at-large Selection Process:
* Online Application (to reduce the need to handle ineligible applications, a rule can be established where must be logged in with student account)
* Brief (e.g., 100-word) description of their experiences at the university and how they believe they would excel in this role
* Brief description of their other commitments (school, work, etc.) during the hiring/selection process
* Name, email, and relationship description of 2 professional contacts (e.g., work supervisor or volunteer supervisor), including at least one from Brock
* Advertised through many channels (social media, email to clubs, student newspaper memo, etc.)

Proposed candidate screening process:
1.	Student Union contacts Registrar to confirm academic eligibility.
2.	Committee reviews resumes & cover letters.
3.	Student Union contacts references and professional staff provides the committee with a summarized version of any relevant feedback.
4.	Committee holds first round of interviews.
5.	Committee short-lists best candidates for live interview.
6.	Committee runs interviews with shortlisted candidates.
