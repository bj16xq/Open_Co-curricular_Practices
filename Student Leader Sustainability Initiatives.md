# Background 

This document outlines various ways in which student leaders can promote environmental sustainability. As a student leader, one reason for introducing sustainability initiatives is because the climate crisis is likely on the minds of some of your peers. In some cases, it may affect their mental health. Rather than fixating on negativity, it is important to recognize the agency that we have by participating in positive change in our communities. This sense of agency is crucial in alleviating anxiety around the climate crisis, which affects a portion of the student population.

# Content: Checklist 
Try to pursue as many of the following items as you can. Share your successes with others, and consider remixing this resource by adapting the content based on new contexts or new knowledge you acquire as you learn more about sustainability and environmental stewardship. 
* Establish a GOOS paper bin in your department, teaching assistant office, etc.
* Investigate the opportunity to change default search engine to “[Ecosia](https://www.ecosia.org/)” on school computers wherever possible;
* Discuss with your department the possibility of adopting an unplugging policy; hypothesize and debate what the language could look like (see “Execution” section)
* Reach out to faculty in the field of sustainability to gain a greater understanding of how to promote sustainable practices on campus;
* Talk to department about strategically buying green products (while avoiding greenwashing) such as: recycled paper, eco-friendly dish soap, compostable utensils;
* Buy from sustainable brands for giveaway prizes (e.g., club events): [tentree](https://www.tentree.com/), [4ocean](https://www.4ocean.com/), etc.;
* Talk to science faculty members about pursuing the [My Green Lab certification](https://mygreenlab.org/programs/mgl-certification/); this organization has a newsletter;

# Execution
## GOOS Paper Bin
GOOS (Good on one side) refers to paper that has only been used on one side, leaving the other side blank and free to use for making notes, sketches, etc. Instead of recycling a paper with an unused side, it is best to leave it in a GOOS paper bin so that someone can use it for notes/sketches, etc. instead of using a brand new piece of paper. Therefore, this process reduces paper consumption. Keep in mind that paper with any confidential information should not be put in a GOOS bin and shredded instead.

## Ecosia
This website is a search engine that operates similar to Google, but as a non-profit, they donate the money they generate from ads toward planting trees and other environmentally-friendly initiatives.

## Unplugging Policy
This means that there is a consistent practice of shutting down and unplugging desktop computers every week or month. This may take place where an Administrative Coordinator sends out a weekly/monthly email reminder.

The reasons for doing this are as follows: (a) device longevity; (b) performance; (c) financial savings (d) environmental impact.
