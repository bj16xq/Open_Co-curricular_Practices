## Background
The ability for student unions to thrive is largely dependent on the leadership composed of student representatives, including the board of directors. To be an effective board, a clear plan for the year is beneficial.

## Proposed Content

The table on the next page consists of a template year-at-a-glance which is partially filled out with basic items that are likely to be common across student unions. The first column lists the meeting number as well as the month and date. The second column is bifurcated to contain information on (i) when members must submit items and material to be included in that month’s agenda, and (ii) when the agenda will be released. The third column lists planned agenda items that coordinated to address crucial responsibilities throughout the year in a maximally seamless and flexible fashion.
