# Background

The England Football Association's 4-corner athlete development model (see Diouf et al., 2024):
1. **Physical:** physical attributes such as strength, speed, and endurance.
2. **Technical/Tactical:** technical skill refers to *coordinated* movement (motor coordination) such as dribbling; tactical skill refers to *strategic* movement such as field positioning.
3. **Psychological:** mental capacities such as coping with performance errors.
4. **Social:** social skills such as team leadership.

This framework can be translated into esports training.
